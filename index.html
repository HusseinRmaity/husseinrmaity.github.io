01
MENA real estate · n8n · WhatsApp · Claude

Real estate lead response and AI qualification, built for MENA

Problem
In Gulf and Levant real estate the first response wins the deal, and buyers expect it on WhatsApp within minutes. Leads arrive at 11pm, on a Friday, during Ramadan — when no agent is watching. The off-the-shelf fix is SMS-first and English-only: it reads as spam, ignores Arabic, and has no idea that Thursday night is the weekend in Riyadh or that a message shouldn’t land mid-prayer.
Solution
A lead arrives from a web form, Property Finder, Bayut or a Meta ad. Within 60 seconds it is validated, deduplicated, language-detected and intent-scored, then answered on WhatsApp in the lead’s own language — Arabic, English or French. Send timing respects the country’s weekend, quiet hours and prayer times. The assistant qualifies over up to six turns (budget, currency, timeline, area, buy or sell), never assumes a currency, then scores the lead, creates a HubSpot contact and deal, and drops a ready-to-call card into the agents’ Slack. No data entry at any point.
Stack
n8n · Twilio WhatsApp · Claude Haiku 4.5 + Sonnet 5 · Supabase · HubSpot · Slack
Result
Self-initiated build, proven end to end on a real phone: form submit to WhatsApp reply inside 60 seconds, a six-turn Arabic conversation, and a scored lead landing in HubSpot and Slack unattended. Built to an 8-point production standard — all 47 external calls carry an explicit timeout, retry and failure path; duplicate webhooks collapse to one lead by database constraint; a 19-scenario adversarial suite covers prompt injection, opt-outs, voice notes and concurrent replies. Model choice is cost-engineered: Haiku for classification, Sonnet only for conversation and scoring, roughly 60% cheaper than a Sonnet-only build.

02
Shopify · n8n · WhatsApp · Claude

WhatsApp abandoned-cart recovery for MENA e-commerce

Problem
Shopify's native cart-recovery email gets ignored in MENA: email open rates are low, and Gulf and Levant shoppers live on WhatsApp, not their inbox. A checkout abandoned at 11pm on a Thursday, the Gulf weekend, can sit untouched until someone checks the abandoned-checkouts report. By then, the customer may have bought elsewhere or forgotten the cart entirely.
Solution
When a Shopify checkout is abandoned, a webhook picks it up after a configurable delay. Claude personalizes a WhatsApp recovery message in Arabic or English, then Twilio sends it. Recovery status is tracked in Supabase; if an order is completed before the message goes out, the send stops. No manual list-pulling and no email-only fallback.
Stack
n8n · Shopify Admin API · Twilio WhatsApp · Claude/Anthropic · Supabase
Result
Self-initiated build, tested end to end on a Shopify dev store: checkout abandonment to WhatsApp message sent, with the send stopping cleanly on order completion. Built to the same 8-point production standard as Case 1: external calls have explicit timeouts, retries and failure-path handling; recovery sends are idempotent against duplicate webhook triggers; and success and failure events are logged for visibility rather than failing silently.
